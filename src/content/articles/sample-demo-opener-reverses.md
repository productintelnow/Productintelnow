This is the troubleshooting template. The symptom in the summary is made up so editors can see where a real symptom will sit. It is not a description of your door.

Garage door springs and cables store enough force to injure you. A published article may explain that limit, then point to the manufacturer’s instructions. It will not improvise a spring adjustment. This sample stops at the template.

## What the product block is doing here

The demo opener is listed as `mentioned`. That role means the part appears so a reader can identify the record later. It is not a diagnosis. The inactive price control is not a suggested replacement part.

## Where sourced checks will go

A later revision can add a short list of checks copied from a cited manual, each with a stop condition. Until those sentences have a source, the body stays descriptive and the specification table stays empty.
