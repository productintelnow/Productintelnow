This page shows the best-of template. The two drivers are demo records in the product database. They are not tools you can buy, and nothing here is a rank earned in a garage.

A published roundup will name the jobs the list is for, such as driving lags into a stud or running machine screws in a tight engine bay. It will say who did the work and what was not tested. This sample skips that, on purpose.

## What the cards are allowed to claim

Each product block is filled from `src/data/products.json`. The article only stores the product id, a rank, and a short editor note. Specs that are still unknown stay labeled as placeholders. Prices are not a field on the product, and this template has nowhere to type one.

## What a later revision adds

Replace the demo product ids with verified ids, point each affiliate link at an https URL, and change `status` from `sample` to `published` only after the checks pass. Until then the page stays out of the sitemap.
