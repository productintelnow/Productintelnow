This is the deal template. It can say that a link was checked on a date. It cannot say what the price was, whether the item is in stock, or how large a discount is. Those facts change, and Amazon's associate rules also bar us from showing a price we scraped.

The link row for this page uses the `deals` strategy. The product's own category tag stays on the tools record. One product can have more than one link row when the same item is mentioned from a deal page and a best-of page.

Nothing on this page is a current offer. The driver is fictional.
