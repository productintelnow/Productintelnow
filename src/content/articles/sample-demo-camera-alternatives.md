This is the alternatives template. It assumes a reader arrived with one product in mind and needs a sourced reason to look at another. Both cameras here are Example Brand demo records.

The reason in the alternatives list is the sentence that has to do the work. "It is cheaper" is not a reason this site can print, because price is not stored. "The manual shows on-device storage and the other requires a plan" is the shape of a reason, and only after the manuals are cited.

Until those citations exist, treat the alternate card as a layout preview.
