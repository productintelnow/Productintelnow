This is the compatibility template. Smart-lock mistakes are expensive because they start with a hole in the door. The table on this page is allowed to say "Not verified" and is not allowed to borrow a fit range from a different lock.

A published guide names the bore, the backset, the thickness, the latch type, and the radio. If the manufacturer publishes a paper template, the guide links it. This demo has no template, so the rows stay blank of numbers.

The product card is here so the missing affiliate link is visible. It is not evidence that the lock fits anything.
