This is the calculator template. The widget below divides a shelf width by a bin width and drops the remainder. That is the whole model. It does not know which brand of bin you own, and it will not suggest one.

Measure the clear opening, not the outside of the upright. If a bin has a lip, include the lip in the bin width you type. The result is a count of whole bins. It is not a shopping list.

A published calculator can stay free of affiliate links, like this one, or it can point at verified products in a separate section. It still does not invent a price.
